Terms of Service — Ary Rongel Position Logger

Last updated: October 22, 2026.

These terms describe the intended use and operational responsibilities for Ary Rongel Position Logger.

1. Purpose and authorized users

The application is an operational tool for recording and transferring vessel-position data. It must be used only by users authorized to access the configured navigation source and to handle the resulting files.

2. Operator responsibilities

The operator is responsible for selecting and checking the NMEA source, verifying the accuracy and validity of the navigation data, configuring UTC time correctly, and monitoring recording and upload results.

The operator is responsible for the Google account and OAuth credentials used by the application, including their protection, appropriate authorization and compliance with the organization’s account and data-handling policies.

The operator or administrator decides whether files should be shared, who may receive them, and whether the reporting workflow meets the recipient’s requirements. Creating a file or uploading it to Google Drive does not itself establish a connection with CATS.

3. Navigation and maritime safety

The application does not replace official navigation or maritime safety systems. It must not be used as a primary source for navigation, collision avoidance or safety-critical decisions.

Position records depend on the configured data source and the operational computer. Operators must use the appropriate official systems and procedures for navigation and vessel safety.

4. Availability and limitations

Network interruptions, Google Drive availability or account restrictions, power loss, hardware failures and unavailable or invalid NMEA data may affect recording, transfer or access to files. Continuous availability and complete position coverage are not guaranteed.

The operator must check alerts, confirm delivery when required, maintain appropriate backups and follow the organization’s procedures for failures and missing records.

5. Privacy and Google Drive

The Privacy Policy describes Google authorization, storage, retention, sharing and revocation of access. Google Drive use remains subject to the terms and policies applicable to the authorized Google account.

6. Changes to these terms

These terms may be updated as the project and its operational procedures evolve. The date above identifies the latest update to this page. Operators should review the current terms when deploying a new version or changing the reporting workflow.
